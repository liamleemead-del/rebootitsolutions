About Me

Professional Personal Service

I was interested in Technology from an early age and was lucky enough when still at school to be offered an apprenticeship where I was able to continue my studies and gain some real life experience.

Over the last 30 years I have been lucky enough to work for some large Blue Chip Companies. In 2008 I decided to set up my own consultancy business (Learn PC) and have continued to enhance my skill set from large scale computing to home and small business computer.

I offer a variety of services from training people on different software to more hardware related problems. I support both individuals and small companies. No issue is too big or too small!
